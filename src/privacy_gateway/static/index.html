Detect locally
Regex, checksums, allow/deny lists, Presidio, and pluggable recognizers produce explicit confidence and provenance.

          
02
Apply policy
Deterministic precedence turns every finding into a visible keep, redact, tokenize, hash, generalize, or synthetic decision.

          
03
Cross safely
Only protected data reaches the external model, API, agent tool, webhook, or analytics system.

          
04
Restore deliberately
Client capsules or encrypted vault mappings restore approved output while queryable audit records explain every decision.
