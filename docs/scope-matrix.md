## Explicit non-goals for the first stable release

- Formal legal certification or a claim that pseudonymization is anonymous.
- Guaranteed detection of all sensitive or identifying information.
- Differential privacy without a separately specified and tested mechanism/budget.
- Training or bundling SDV or reimplementing its private/internal architecture.
- Claiming production image protection before an image adapter passes Stage 1.
- Supporting arbitrary proxy upstream URLs supplied per request.
